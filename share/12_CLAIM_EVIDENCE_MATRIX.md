# Capability–evidence matrix

This matrix maps each implemented capability to its verification evidence. The system's architecture — Jetson Orin Nano with TensorRT-accelerated perception, ROS 2 mission coordination, Mac-backed depth/segmentation/cloud inference, and EV3 low-level motor control — enables these capabilities. Refer to the [full dossier](FULL_TECHNICAL_DOSSIER.md) and [snapshot](SOURCE_SNAPSHOT.md) for provenance.
